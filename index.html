    Questions, answered.

    Still unsure? Join the waitlist and reply to the welcome email.

    How is Vertlix different from a single AI assistant?

    An assistant answers prompts. Vertlix coordinates many agents toward a goal, with structure, spending limits and approvals around them.

    Can I bring my existing agents?

    Yes. Connect any model, script or service that can receive a request from Vertlix. Adapters handle the rest.

    What happens when an agent hits its budget?

    You get a warning at 80%. At 100% the agent pauses and new tasks are held until you raise the limit or say otherwise.

    Do agents run all the time?

    By default they wake on a schedule or when assigned work. You can also connect agents that run continuously.

    Can I run more than one business?

    Yes. Each company gets its own isolated workspace, data and budgets.
